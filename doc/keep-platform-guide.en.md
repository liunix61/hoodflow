## Platform Overview

### 🎯 What is Keep?

**Keep** is a token issuance platform for AI builders, based on **Solana** blockchain.

**Core Features**:
- ✅ Token Launch (token issuance)
- ✅ Keepedia (project discovery and community valuation)
- ✅ Points (points system)

**Key Features**:
- 🛡️ Backer refund protection (price protection)
- 🔒 Parameters immutable (locked at deploy time)
- 💰 Liquidity non-withdrawable
- 📝 Editable with full traceability

### 💰 Price Protection Mechanism

**How It Works**:

1. **Backer Protection Period**: 24-72 hours after launch
2. **Price Floor**: Launch price + 10%
3. **Automatic Calculation**: Based on actual trading volume
4. **Refund Trigger**: If price drops below floor

**Example**:
- Launch Price: 0.01 SOL
- Price Floor: 0.011 SOL (10% above launch)
- Backer Protection Period: 24 hours
- If trading volume < 100 SOL, backers get refund

## Price Protection Mechanism

### 🛡️ Detailed Explanation

**Price Protection Features**:

1. **Backer Refund Guarantee**
   - Backers are protected for 24-72 hours
   - Refund if price drops below floor

2. **Immutable Parameters**
   - Parameters locked at deploy time
   - No modifications after deployment

3. **Liquidity Non-withdrawable**
   - Liquidity locked for 6 months
   - No withdrawal during protection period

4. **Full Traceability**
   - Every edit is recorded
   - On-chain verification

## FAQ

### ❓ Common Questions

#### Q1: What is Price Protection?

**A**: Price protection guarantees that backers get a refund if the token price drops below a certain threshold within the protection period.

#### Q2: Can I modify launch parameters after deployment?

**A**: No, parameters are immutable and locked at deploy time.

#### Q3: How long is the liquidity locked?

**A**: Liquidity is locked for 6 months.

#### Q4: What wallets are supported?

**A**: Phantom, Solflare, Sollet, Backpack.

#### Q5: How is token price determined?

**A**: Token price is determined by trading volume on the Keep platform.

#### Q6: Can I withdraw liquidity before 6 months?

**A**: No, liquidity is non-withdrawable for 6 months.
